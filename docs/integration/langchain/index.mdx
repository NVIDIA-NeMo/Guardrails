This section covers how to integrate the NeMo Guardrails library with LangChain and LangGraph.

<Warning>

The LangChain integration, including `RunnableRails` and the LangChain agent middleware, is deprecated in 0.25.0 and is removed in 0.27.0 (January 2027).
Call `Guardrails` directly from your chain, agent, or graph instead.
For the steps, refer to [Migrating to 0.25](/reference/0-25#langchain-chains-and-agents).

</Warning>
